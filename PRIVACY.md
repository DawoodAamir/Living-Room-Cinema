# Privacy

Favorites and viewing positions are stored locally on Apple TV. The app has no account, analytics, advertising, external AI service, or proprietary backend.

Bundled motion studies play offline. Selecting the Apple HLS example sends ordinary streaming requests directly to Apple’s CDN, which receives network request information such as your IP address. The app does not download that stream into its own persistent library.

Clear viewing history removes local bookmarks while keeping favorites. tvOS can purge app storage; this app provides no cloud backup. Device-level playback and network settings remain controlled by the system.
